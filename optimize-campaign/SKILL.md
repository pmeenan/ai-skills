# Optimize benchmark campaigns

One campaign = one frozen baseline, one rendering surface, one calibration
epoch, one `ledger.json`, stored on the test machine. `campaign.py` is the
only writer of campaign state and forwards itself to that host when this
checkout is linked to it (`campaign.py link-remote`); every gate is a machine
check. Read the benchmark adapter
(`../optimize-speedometer` or `../optimize-jetstream`) first, then
[measurement-policy.md](references/measurement-policy.md). Commands are in
[campaign-runbook.md](references/campaign-runbook.md); transport in
[execution.md](references/execution.md); human prompts in [README.md](README.md).

## Operating modes

A campaign runs in one of two operating modes, chosen at `campaign.py init --mode single-agent|multi-engine` (recorded in `ledger.json` as `config.operating_mode`, default `single-agent`) or by a documented convention in the campaign's `CLAUDE.md`. Nothing in `campaign.py` or the gates requires a mailbox.

- **Single-agent (default):** One orchestrator agent runs the campaign and owns `ledger.json` and all **orchestrator-only (in multi-engine mode, the host)** gate and ledger actions (`decompose` import, `advance`, `park`, `review`, `note`, `register-site`, `open-phase`, `exclude-hypothesis`, `set-feature`, `reserve-key`, `budget`, `hold`, and `evidence/invalidated_manifests.json` rulings). It delegates investigation, decomposition drafting, measurement, implementation, and gate reviews to subagents.
- **Multi-engine (optional):** Separate **host** (orchestrator) and **operator** agents/engines coordinate through the file-based mailbox (`outbox/mailbox/PROTOCOL.md`, `outbox/mailbox/wait_for.sh`, `to-operator/ROUND-<N>.prompt.md`, `to-host/ROUND-<N>.report.md`, strictly sequential round numbers, atomic `.tmp` + `mv` writes, one final `ROUND-<N>.report.md` per round, `HOLD` prompts, and `STOP` sentinel).
- **Independence in single-agent mode:** Gate reviewers (`skeptic` and `adversary`) are always fresh, read-only subagents with clean context, spawned from prompts the orchestrator writes. They are never the author of the artifact and are never reused after an artifact change (`reviews/gate-report-registry.json` enforces that a `reviewer_task_id` cannot reappear with different `artifact_digests_checked`). The orchestrator verifies all subagent output from disk before acting on it. Report integrity and evidence integrity apply to the orchestrator and to every subagent.

## The loop, in order

1. **Resume or init.** If `.agents/campaigns/current/ledger.json` exists: `status --print`, `audit`, continue from the recorded gate. Otherwise `init` with the frozen baseline, the host's display policy (`--display :1 --display-vt 9 --pause-service ollama` on the Linux box) and the fleet bot. Never create a second campaign, manifest or ledger to get past a gate.
2. **Calibrate.** Two separately timed A/A runs on the campaign surface, then `campaign.py calibrate`. This records each story's minimum detectable effect (MDE); the qualification floor for a story becomes twice its MDE. Nothing below that floor is worth a build. `profile` refuses to import without a calibration record (`--allow-uncalibrated` records a placeholder-floor discovery profile and says so in STATUS).
3. **Profile.** Two independent exact-window captures (32 reps, fixed-period sampling, same display). Frontiers rank **renderer main-thread** work per story; every entry carries a portability flag and every story a score-time composition (sync vs async, busy vs idle). Then run `story_lens.py` on both captures and import it with `profile --lens`: it decomposes every story into triggers (forced layout by JS entry API, frame update, hit-test lifecycle), lifecycle phases, ownership (Blink-addressable vs V8/JS hand-off), profiler overhead, and the hotspots nested inside each frontier entry. `profile-scaffold` pairs parent/child root substitutions across captures instead of dropping them; the two profile-gate reviews come from `profile-review-scaffold` and must cite a number per check, with their transcripts copied to the ledger host.
4. **Investigate one story area.** Read `investigator.md`. Start from the lens: the trigger and phase mix, the JS API that forced the work, and the nested hotspots marked as a different phase than their parent (those get their own decomposition rows). Before proposing a Layer 1/2 mechanism, run the redundancy probe on the site and cite its packet; the claimed avoidable fraction may not exceed what the counts support. Route the hypothesis review to the strongest available model. Work the V8/JS hand-off share as `out-of-scope` rows that name the owner, never as silence.
5. **Decompose and qualify.** `decompose` refuses impacts below the story floor and Layer 1/2 claims without redundancy evidence. Reviews must cite artifacts and numbers. The discovery milestone is the **vetted candidate list** in [vetted-candidates.md](references/vetted-candidates.md): every story's addressable share above floor closed by a candidate packet, a counted `no-qualifying-mechanism`, a `mandatory` invariant or a `handoff` row, **and** `campaign.py depth-audit` empty: a count on an ancestor closes a row for the ancestor's hypothesis only, so every phase whose rows above the floor rest on an ancestor's count has had each class in [hypotheses.md](references/hypotheses.md) probed on its own function in that story (a packet tagged `--hypothesis-class`) or excluded by the orchestrator (in multi-engine mode, the host). Each round ends with the audit; its open entries are the next counter proposals. If the campaign carries a review hold, stop here: run `export-candidates`, report, and wait for a human `hold --release`; sizing and implementation are refused until then.
6. **Exhaust the three searches.** Discovery is three phases in order, each opened by the orchestrator (in multi-engine mode, the host) with `open-phase` once the previous is exhausted: **redundancy** (the story frontiers, by count), **suite** (`suite-frontier --open`: functions below every story floor whose suite mean clears the suite floor, decomposed at the suite floor), **efficiency** (`efficiency-frontier --open`: the counted, necessary work ranked by the time it carries itself; then `own-time-frontier --open`: every in-scope function, counted or not, whose own body clears a story floor; each area's one row is `algorithmic` with a cost packet or `no-qualifying-mechanism` with an investigation quoting it). `algorithmic` rows are refused before the efficiency phase, and one below both floors is refused in it: the big redundancies are searched first, and micro-optimizations are never candidates. See "Discovery phases" in [decomposition.md](references/decomposition.md).
7. **Size.** Instrumented twin, three or more targeted blocks per arm, `mechanism_evidence.py`; the avoidable-share lower bound must clear the story floor.
8. **Implement, review, measure.** One invariant behind the campaign flag, build/test receipts, local code review, then the fixed-plan A/B on the same surface. Story flags in manifests are family-adjusted; unadjusted flags are noise until confirmed by a preregistered run.
9. **Land, checkpoint, reprofile.** Landed work is banked only with a local fixed-plan IMPROVEMENT manifest and a Pinpoint IMPROVEMENT on the campaign bot; targeted and full-suite checkpoints follow the adapter cadence.

## Where the model's effort goes

- **Mechanical measurement and extraction** (captures, A/B blocks, `ingest`/`summarize`, build/test receipts, digest tables): a light, cheap model is fine. Follow the runbook commands literally and paste real outputs.
- **Investigation, drafting, implementation and gate reviews:** use the strongest available model — turning a story frontier plus composition into ranked hypotheses, drafting decompositions, implementing candidate invariants, go/no-go sizing decisions, and independent skeptic/adversary gate reviews and semantic/spec reviews.
- **Configurable concurrency and SSH fragility:** Keep subagent concurrency limits configurable (`--max-concurrent-subagents` at `init`, `config.max_concurrent_subagents`, default `4`). When subagents work against a remote host over SSH, enforce **one SSH connection per subagent**, **no parallel SSH invocations inside a subagent**, and **at most about 4 concurrent remote subagents**.
- Spend creativity on *why the work runs* (repeated inputs, invalidation, phases that could be skipped, work off the score path), not on the leaf that happens to be hot. A leaf tweak that cannot clear the story floor is not a candidate.

## Evidence rules that are enforced by code

- Rendering surface (headless or X display), viewport and GPU renderer are recorded in every manifest and must match the frozen campaign policy and calibration; a run on the wrong surface is rejected at import.
- Story silos are renderer main-thread only; 100 nominal samples at the floor per story or the capture is rejected (raise repetitions or sampling rate, never the gate).
- Qualification floor = max(share floor, 2 × story MDE). Sizing must clear the same floor.
- Layer 1/2 proposals bind a digest-checked redundancy packet.
- PASS reviews cite an existing artifact or bound digest plus a number, with distinct evidence per check. Profile-gate reviews use the `profile-review-scaffold` checks and decomposition-gate reviews the `decompose-review-scaffold` checks (probe key vs hypothesis, floor arithmetic, existing mechanism); every gate review's transcript must resolve on the ledger host (`--gate-<role>-transcript`). A reviewer report attests one artifact digest set; a reused task id with different digests is refused. `decompose` closes `mandatory`/`no-qualifying-mechanism` rows at or above the story floor only by a bound count (`share × supported fraction < floor`) or `wrapper_of` a counted row, and a `novel` row names its `existing_mechanism`.
- Recurrence across captures is decided on sample coverage: an entry rooted at a parent in one capture and at its child in the other is one area (`recurrence: root-substitution`), verified from both inventories at import.
- A wrong profile import is withdrawn with `profile-retract` and re-imported under a new id; ledger rows are never edited and ids are never reused.
- Score runs keep ASLR on; cycle-probe captures turn it off. The tuner owns VT handoff and clock policy and restores everything.
- Balanced ABBA/BAAB blocks are the acceptance unit; Bonferroni bounds over all stories plus the suite; no selective reruns.
- Build/test receipts only from `command_evidence.py`; mechanism rows only from nonce-bound captures; landing evidence recomputed from runner manifests and digest-bound in the ledger.
- **Evidence integrity:** Never move, rename, or hide files under `evidence/` (such as `evidence/_archived*`) to pass `precheck_decomposition.py` or a gate; invalidating superseded or bad evidence requires an orchestrator-only (in multi-engine mode, the host) ruling recorded in `evidence/invalidated_manifests.json`. This rule applies to the orchestrator and to every subagent.
- **Benchmark special-casing disallowed:** Designs that pattern-match page JavaScript source or benchmark-specific code shape (for example `#415` Component A matching `if (oldValue === newValue) return;` in framework glue) are disallowed and cannot be used to clear qualification floors or land.
- **Report integrity:** Campaign and round reports (written by the orchestrator or any subagent) must source every fact from same-turn disk-reading scripts, print `"NOT CHECKED"` when a fact is not read from disk in that turn, never hardcode prose claims or fallback numbers in report generators, and never complete or guess git/sha256 hash prefixes.

## Stop and report when

A/A calibration fails its gate; the capture cannot reach the sample floor on the configured surface; a mechanism cannot clear its story floor; a candidate's family-adjusted regression survives; or the fresh main-thread frontier holds nothing above the floors. Say which gate stopped you and what number missed by how much. Never invent an exhaustion claim.
